# User Guide

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Application
1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to `http://localhost:8080` (or your deployed URL)
3. Log in with your credentials

### First-Time Setup
1. Contact your administrator to create your account
2. You will receive an email with your login credentials
3. Log in and change your password
4. Complete your profile information

## Features

### Team Management

#### Viewing Teams
1. Click on "Teams" in the navigation menu
2. You will see a list of teams you have access to
3. Click on a team to view its details

#### Team Details
- Team name and description
- List of team members
- Upcoming events
- Team calendar

### Event Management

#### Creating an Event
1. Navigate to the team's page
2. Click "Create Event"
3. Fill in the event details:
   - Title
   - Date and time
   - Location
   - Description
4. Click "Save"

#### Managing Events
- View all events in the calendar view
- Filter events by team
- Export events to Google Calendar
- Set reminders for events

### User Management

#### Profile Management
1. Click on your profile picture
2. Select "Profile"
3. Update your information:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Phone number
   - Profile picture

#### Password Management
1. Go to "Settings"
2. Select "Change Password"
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter and confirm your new password
5. Click "Save"

## Calendar Integration

### Google Calendar Setup
1. Click on "Settings"
2. Select "Calendar Integration"
3. Click "Connect Google Calendar"
4. Follow the OAuth prompts
5. Select which calendars to sync

### Calendar Features
- View team events in your Google Calendar
- Receive notifications for upcoming events
- Sync event changes automatically
- Share team calendars with other members

## Notifications

### Setting Up Notifications
1. Go to "Settings"
2. Select "Notifications"
3. Configure your preferences:
   - Email notifications
   - In-app notifications
   - Calendar reminders

### Notification Types
- Event reminders
- Team updates
- New member notifications
- Schedule changes

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Problems
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify your username and password
3. Try resetting your password
4. Contact your administrator if issues persist

#### Calendar Sync Issues
1. Check your Google Calendar connection
2. Verify your calendar permissions
3. Try reconnecting your Google account
4. Clear your browser cache

#### Event Creation Problems
1. Verify you have the correct permissions
2. Check if the event time is available
3. Ensure all required fields are filled
4. Try creating the event in a different browser
